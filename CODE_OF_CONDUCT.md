# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming and respectful of differing viewpoints.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith where reasonable.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its technical merits.
- Respect privacy; do not publish personal information or secrets.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on the repository or owner's GitHub profile. The project owner may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate these standards.
